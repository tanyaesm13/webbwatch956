🎶 Submit Your Music

La Onda Libre is a completely independent, completely indie internet radio station — no labels, no gatekeepers, broadcasting 24/7 from Laredo, Texas. We exist to showcase independent artists who own their music, with royalty-free promotional airplay on a bilingual station that loves discovering new sounds.

How it works

  1. Tap the gold button below — the submission form takes about a minute.
  2. Upload one track or paste a link to your music (SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Drive, Dropbox…).
  3. Confirm the music is yours and clean — that's it. Our Music Director reviews every submission.
  4. If your track fits the station, we email you and it goes into rotation. Free — always.
🎶 Open the Submission Form
The house rules
  • You must own your music (or have the rights to share it for airplay) — you'll confirm this on the form.
  • Clean lyrics only — no profanity or explicit content. Every track is screened before airplay.
  • All genres welcome — jazz, pop, Latin, rock, country, electronic, folk, worship, world and more.
  • Our vibe: pleasant vocals and feel-good music — no screaming, no hard metal.

Submissions are reviewed by Nova, Music Director of La Onda Libre. Submitting grants the station permission for free promotional airplay of the tracks you send. Questions: webbwatch956@outlook.com

🎶 Manda tu música

La Onda Libre es una estación de radio por internet completamente independiente — sin disqueras ni intermediarios, transmitiendo las 24 horas desde Laredo, Texas. Mostramos artistas independientes que son dueños de su música, con difusión promocional gratuita en una estación bilingüe.

Cómo funciona: toca el botón dorado, llena el formulario (toma un minuto), sube tu canción o pega un enlace, y confirma que la música es tuya y limpia. Nuestro director musical revisa cada envío. Si tu canción encaja con la estación, te escribimos por correo y entra a la rotación. Gratis — siempre.

Reglas: debes ser dueño de tu música · letra limpia, sin groserías ni contenido explícito · todos los géneros bienvenidos · nuestra onda: voces agradables y música que se siente bien — sin gritos, sin metal pesado.
